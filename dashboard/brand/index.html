Using the assets

Use the square icon for apps and browser tabs. Use the dark logo on light backgrounds and the light logo on dark backgrounds. Keep the original proportions and leave clear space around the mark. Banners are 1600 × 480; the social-card layout is 1200 × 630.

All reusable assets are SVG. Wordmarks use a system-font stack and may vary slightly by device. Social platforms that require raster uploads need a PNG export; the social-card SVG is a source layout, not a universal upload format. Native Mac .icns packaging is not included.

The original generated proposal and identity notes are in the branding material. This identity remains a concept and has not undergone trademark clearance.

Your sources

Where farmy reads from: the folders on your farm computer, the hub sensors, one outside service, and your messages. For e-mail, SMS and WhatsApp, you choose which senders get in at all. Anything you leave out is never read, not even by the copilot on the hub.

Who may see which source

For each recipient: Off, Pseudonymised (names, numbers and links replaced by roles before anything leaves) or Full. The copilot asks your wallet about every item before sending it. Guarantee is what actually protects the data: the hub enforces it, a contract promises it, or nothing does.
